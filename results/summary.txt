*** DO NOT TREAT THESE AS PHYSICALLY VALID NUMBERS. ***
Two independent red flags on this run:

1. y+ on the wing surface: min 82.6, max 2089.5, average 456 - see
   yPlus1 function object output. This is far outside both the resolved-
   sublayer range (~1) and the standard wall-function range (~30-300)
   this case's BCs assume. Root cause: snappyHexMesh added ZERO boundary-
   layer cells on this mesh (confirmed in log.snappyHexMesh: "Writing 0
   faces inside added layer") despite MESHING_NOTES.md describing an
   intended ~1 mm/12-layer stack - the near-wall cells are just the raw
   background/refinement hex cells, roughly 2 orders of magnitude too
   large for U=102 m/s.

2. Cl = -1.69 is not physically plausible for a 3.06 deg AoA wing.
   Thin-airfoil theory gives Cl ~ 2*pi*alpha(rad) ~ 0.34 for the 2D
   section; a finite swept wing would reduce that further, not flip its
   sign or push it past -1. The pressure-side/suction-side force
   resolved through a wall-function BC that assumes a valid log-law
   region - which does not exist at y+~450-2000 - is not trustworthy.

3. Convergence: U, k, omega residuals reached ~1e-5, but the controlling
   p residual plateaued at ~1.0-1.5e-4 for the last several hundred
   iterations (never met the 1e-5 residualControl target) - the run used
   its full 2000-iteration budget rather than stopping early. This is
   consistent with (not fully independent of) the near-wall breakdown
   above feeding noise into the pressure correction.

Recommendation: rebuild the mesh so snappyHexMesh actually adds boundary-
layer cells (the meshQualityControls/layer settings that produced the
zero-coverage result need retuning - same failure mode seen on the
CylinderV2 case in this project), re-target y+ into the wall-function
range (or resolve it with y+~1 and switch to a low-Re-capable BC set),
then re-run. Until then, treat this Cd/Cl pair as a diagnostic check of
the setup, not a design number.
